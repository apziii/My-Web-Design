About Mendi Secondary School

Education shaped by purpose, community and hope.

Our history

History

The school was established to expand access to quality secondary education in the Southern Highlands and to give young people a place to learn close to home.

Since its establishment, Mendi Secondary School has served the community by preparing students for further study, work and service while respecting the culture and needs of Papua New Guinea.

“We nurture the future.”

Our motto reflects the care, guidance and opportunity we give to each student.
